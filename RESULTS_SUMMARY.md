#### Decision & Methodological Clarification
- **DO NOT ADOPT (Null Result)**: The intensity target was to be adopted only if the Large-quartile out-of-fold $R^2$ improved without degrading overall model performance. Because the Large quartile degraded sharply from -0.2656 to -0.5819 (drop of -0.3163), the absolute target formulation was retained.
- **Discrepancy Note (Large-Quartile Absolute $R^2$ = -0.2656 vs -0.0470 in Segment Reliability):**
  - The intensity experiment was evaluated strictly on the **valid revenue subset ($n=563$)**, where intensity (`log_scope3 - log_revenue`) can be computed. On this subset, absolute Large-quartile $R^2$ is **-0.2656**.
  - The segment reliability analysis was evaluated on the **full cross-validation dataset ($n=1,069$)**, where out-of-fold predictions for companies with and without financials were generated together and subsequently segmented, yielding **-0.0470** for the Large revenue bucket under 5-fold CV.

### Data Integrity and Audit Protocol

- **Data Provenance:** Training and validation emissions data are self-reported company disclosures (CDP / ADEME via NZDPU), not audited or third-party verified disclosures. All references to 'Verified' have been removed.
- **Zero Imputation:** No financial values or emissions disclosures were fabricated or synthetically imputed.
- **Independent Validation Integrity:** COGEM companies overlapping with training data (BP, Uniper, Volkswagen) are explicitly partitioned and excluded from headline error statistics.
- **Optimizer Basis Consistency:** Unified strictly to factor-based emissions across API, backend, and dashboard.
